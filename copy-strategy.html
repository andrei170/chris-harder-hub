Foundation

Chris Harder | Copy Strategy

Prepared 2026-05-16 by AG1 Consulting Ltd for @chriswharder

The voice, the framing, the headline bank, and the avatar map that all surfaces of the Chris Harder funnel write to. This doc is the foundation copy lock; every page (homepage VSL script, EEM apply-page VSL, MCM apply-page VSL, scorecard intro copy, segmented welcome flow, sprint sales page) writes downstream of these decisions.

1. Core Voice Register

Generosity-coded operator voice. Direct, plain-language, results-oriented. Warmer than the average business-coach brand. Faith-adjacent without being sermon-coded. Money-as-tool, not money-as-vanity. Family-and-business-blended; Chris and Lori are a duo, not two solo brands.

What the voice sounds like

  • Confident without being puffed up
  • Plain language about money, no euphemism
  • Outcome-named, not feature-named
  • Light reference to generosity / impact at the close of a thought, not as the lead
  • Couple-coded where natural (Chris and Lori, "we," "what we built")

What the voice does not sound like

  • Leverage-stack guru register (vague mass-noun "freedom + leverage" stacking)
  • Law-of-Attraction register ("call it in," vague abundance claims)
  • Sermonising about generosity (the audience already buys the philosophy, the funnel does not need to re-sell it)
  • Agency-coach register ("scaled to seven figures with frameworks and systems")
  • Hedge-fund-analyst jargon
  • Corporate softness register

2. Brand Pillars

  1. Good people make good money. Money is a tool, the operator is the moral centre, the outcomes go to family + community + cause.
  2. Mentorship over marketplace. The room composition is the product. Chris and Lori are in the room every week; the AI Clone Coaching extends it to 24/7; the expert-coach office hours layer it deeper.
  3. The blueprint, the ladder, the room. Three nouns the brand keeps returning to. Blueprint = the free download. Ladder = the course-to-mastermind progression. Room = the mastermind cohort itself.
  4. The podcast is the relationship. The Chris Harder Show is the indoctrination engine + the relationship layer. Every paid surface should write like it knows the reader has heard a hundred episodes.

3. Avatar Map

The four buyer avatars across the funnel. Each surface targets one or more.

Avatar A: Side-Hustle Starter / Pre-$25K Beginner

  • Reads: BeOnline, The Money Principles, podcast back catalogue, blueprint
  • Bottleneck: offer, traffic, mindset
  • Lane: Lane A in the segmented welcome flow
  • Voice modifier: more permission-giving, more "here is the first step," less ladder-positioning

Avatar B: $25K-$500K Operator / MCM-Fit

  • Reads: MCM page, podcast, blueprint, eventually the scorecard
  • Bottleneck: scaling past the ceiling, hiring the first 1-3 people, building the first proper funnel
  • Lane: Lane B in the segmented welcome flow
  • Voice modifier: mentorship-coded, room-coded, expert-coach-office-hours-coded

Avatar C: $500K-$5M Scaling Operator / EEM-Fit

  • Reads: EEM page (mastermindharder.com), podcast, blueprint, scorecard
  • Bottleneck: scaling past 7-figures, team build, brand-as-leverage
  • Lane: Lane C in the segmented welcome flow
  • Voice modifier: room-composition-coded ("6, 7, and 8-figure entrepreneurs in the same room"), in-person-event-coded, quarterly-1-on-1-coded

Avatar D: 1-on-1 Coaching Buyer

  • Reads: 1-on-1 page, has likely listened to Chris specifically for a long time
  • Bottleneck: a specific deal, a specific pivot, a specific high-stakes decision
  • Lane: flagged in the scorecard, separately routed
  • Voice modifier: private, decision-coded, no-room-context

6. Voice Lock (Hard Rules On Every Surface)

The voice is plain-language, generosity-coded, operator-direct. The "Add some abundance to your inbox" live brand phrase stays. Em dashes never appear in body copy except as the sign-off separator ("- Andrei"). Triple-adjective lists are out. "Not just X, but Y" construction is out. Consecutive short-burst sentences read as agency-coach register, not Chris Harder register, so they are out. Named peer-tier operators do not appear in body copy; "peer-tier creators in the mastermind category" is the abstraction when comparison is needed. Dollar amounts do not appear in landing-page body copy; price tiers stay at hint-range level on the landing surface and exact prices live on the application form for the cycle the operator applies to. Every draft passes a voice check against the three live brand phrases in section 7 before it ships.

8. Voice Calibration Examples (Same Idea, Three Registers)

Idea: the scorecard segments applicants before they reach the team.

  • Off-brand (agency-coach register): "We installed a 12-question filter to maximise close-rate efficiency on EEM applications and 10x your team's productivity."
  • Off-brand (Law-of-Attraction register): "The Money Multiplier Score helps you tune into where you are on your journey and align with the right room for you."
  • On-brand (Chris): "Twelve questions. A 0-100 score. Three lines on what to fix first. Then the application gets routed to the right room."

Idea: the holding-pattern sprint sits between MCM cycles.

  • Off-brand (agency-coach register): "The 4-week sprint is a paid pre-qualification mechanism that maximises MCM cohort fill rates."
  • Off-brand (Law-of-Attraction register): "The sprint is your bridge cycle, the next step on your wealth journey."
  • On-brand (Chris): "The MCM cohort opens twice a year. The sprint is the four weeks in between. Chris and Lori, live, fifty seats."
