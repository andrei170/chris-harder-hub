Phase 3

Chris Harder | Content Strategy

Prepared 2026-05-16 by AG1 Consulting Ltd for @chriswharder

The post-install content plan. Three content lanes, a 40-hook bank, a long-form plan, and a photoshoot brief. The content plan does not change the podcast cadence; the podcast is the relationship engine and runs untouched. This plan is the content layer that feeds the new funnel.

Three Content Lanes

Lane 1: Episode Companions (IG + Email)

One per podcast episode. Goes to the segmented welcome flow (the lane-appropriate cadence) on the day the episode drops.

The Chris Harder Show drops on cadence. Every episode is the seed for a one-image carousel + a one-paragraph email tied to the right lane. The lane-routing logic:

  • Episode topic is beginner / mindset / money-philosophy → Lane A (under $25K) gets the email
  • Episode topic is scaling / first hires / first funnel → Lane B (MCM-fit) gets the email
  • Episode topic is 8-figure / team / exit / brand-as-leverage → Lane C (EEM-fit) gets the email
  • Topic-neutral or interview episode → All three lanes get a lane-tuned cut of the same email

The companion content does not just summarise the episode. It pulls one decision the operator can make this week, tied to the lane-appropriate next step (course, MCM scorecard prompt, EEM scorecard prompt).

Lane 2: Funnel-Surfacing Posts (IG + LinkedIn)

Two per week. Direct response to the funnel infrastructure.

These posts exist to surface the new funnel pieces (scorecard, sprint, segmented welcome flow). Not the podcast. Not the masterminds. The funnel itself.

  • Sample post #1: a screen-record of the scorecard, with the operator's actual question prompts visible. "Twelve questions. 0-100 score. Three lines. Take the score."
  • Sample post #2: a behind-the-scenes of the Chris + Lori VSL shoot day. "First time we have shot the room itself, not just the philosophy."
  • Sample post #3: the sprint sales page hero, with the cap callout. "Four weeks, fifty seats, between MCM cycles."
  • Sample post #4: a screenshot of the segmented welcome flow on the ESP backend. "Three lanes, one list. Beginner, MCM-fit, EEM-fit."

Lane 3: Operator Reads (LinkedIn long-form + Newsletter)

One per week. Long-form thought piece, 600-900 words, written under Chris's name.

These pieces feed the EEM-fit audience (Lane C) primarily, with the MCM-fit audience as secondary. The topic bank:

  • The room composition is the product, not the room access
  • Why mentorship beats marketplace at every revenue band
  • The application gate as room protection, not sales filter
  • The bi-weekly cadence as the unit of mentorship
  • Generosity-coded operator brand vs leverage-stack operator brand
  • Couple-coded business brand (Chris + Lori as duo) as moat
  • The 8-figure question: what do you do with the money
  • The MCM-to-EEM ladder, when to move, when to stay
  • The 4-week sprint as warm-list discipline
  • The Money Multiplier Score as application UX

These pieces also seed podcast topics. The cycle: write the long-form, watch which one performs, turn the high-performer into a podcast monologue episode.

Long-Form Plan (Post-Install)

YouTube cadence

  • 1x per month: a 12-18 min "inside the room" episode. Chris + Lori, walking what is actually happening in MCM and EEM that month (with member permission, themes and decisions, not named operations).
  • 1x per quarter: a 25-35 min "sprint debrief" episode. After each sprint cycle, what worked, what shifted, what the cohort produced. Feeds the next sprint cycle.
  • Continued evergreen podcast publishing on existing cadence (do not disrupt The Chris Harder Show).

Newsletter cadence

  • Lane A: 2 emails per week (one episode companion, one course nurture)
  • Lane B: 2 emails per week (one episode companion, one MCM-fit thought piece)
  • Lane C: 2 emails per week (one episode companion, one operator-read long-form excerpt)
  • Cross-lane broadcast: 1 per week, the "what is new across the funnel" digest, optional opt-out

LinkedIn cadence

  • 1x per week: a Lane 3 long-form operator read under Chris's name
  • 2x per week: a Lane 2 funnel-surfacing post
  • Light comment engagement on EEM-fit operator pages (Lori or a community manager handles)

Photoshoot Brief

Goal

A single shoot day in install week or week 2 that produces the brand asset library for the funnel: VSL B-roll, scorecard intro page, sprint sales page hero, segmented welcome flow header, ad creative library, and 6-12 months of organic content stills.

Setup

  • Setting: Chris and Lori's home or studio (whichever is the current podcast background)
  • Two looks (one warm-casual, one slightly elevated)
  • Both Chris and Lori in every set
  • Photographer + lighting + sound (sound for the VSL component)
  • Half day photo only, half day video for VSLs

Shot list (photo)

  1. Two-shot, warm register, against the brand backdrop (homepage hero)
  2. Chris solo, mid-shot, looking at camera (scorecard intro page hero)
  3. Lori solo, mid-shot, looking at camera (MCM apply-page hero)
  4. Two-shot, mid-shot, both looking at a phone or screen (sprint sales page hero)
  5. Two-shot, environmental, in the podcast studio (Lane 1 episode companion graphics)
  6. Wide environmental shots of the studio or office (Lane 2 funnel-surfacing posts)
  7. Detail shots: scorecard on phone, blueprint PDF on iPad, "Add some abundance to your inbox" on phone, the Skool MCM community on a laptop
  8. Black-and-white set for operator-read long-form headers (Lane 3)

Wardrobe

  • Look 1: warm casual, brand-neutral colours, generosity-coded (avoid stiff suits, avoid overly casual)
  • Look 2: elevated, suitable for EEM apply-page hero, still warm

Deliverables out of the shoot

  • 60-90 photo selects, retouched
  • VSL A-roll for three videos (homepage, EEM, MCM)
  • B-roll for ad recuts and sprint sales-page video
  • Detail shots for landing-page heroes across the funnel
  • 6-12 months of organic content stills banked

What This Content Plan Does Not Touch

  • The Chris Harder Show podcast cadence, episode structure, or guest mix (untouched, runs on existing workflow)
  • Lori Harder's own brand or podcast (separate operator, separate funnel)
  • The Frello content stream (separate brand, separate plan)
  • Existing course content (BeOnline, Money Principles stay as-is, not re-shot)
  • The MCM Skool community or EEM in-person event content (member-only, out of scope for public funnel content)

The content plan is the layer that feeds the new funnel surfaces. It does not replace anything currently running. It runs alongside.
