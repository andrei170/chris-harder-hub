Lead Doc

Chris Harder | Bottleneck Analysis

Prepared 2026-05-16 by AG1 Consulting Ltd for @chriswharder

Four things I would build differently on top of the existing offer ladder, ranked by revenue impact. All four are infrastructure layers, none of them are new offers. Full install ships in less than 7 days from kickoff. Money in the bank inside the first sprint cycle.

Bottleneck One: Podcast-To-Application VSL Bridge On EEM, MCM, And The Homepage

What is happening now

chrisharder.me, mastermindharder.com, and chrisharder.me/mentor are all text + static-image pages with one CTA each. No homepage VSL, no embedded application-page VSL, no podcast voice surfacing on any premium-tier sales page. The audience that lands on these pages is podcast-trained, hundreds of hours of Chris and Lori in their ears across The Chris Harder Show catalogue. Audio-first trust meets text-first conversion. The bridge that should carry the relationship across the gap is missing.

Why it matters

A 6-10 minute homepage VSL plus 4-6 minute application-page VSLs on EEM and MCM extends the podcast voice directly into the buy decision. The same audience already knows what Chris and Lori sound like; the VSL turns "I want to learn from these two" into "I am applying today." None of the peer-tier creators in the mastermind category ship pure-text premium-tier sales pages.

Cost of waiting

Conservatively ~$15-40K per month in EEM, MCM, and 1-on-1 application volume lost to the text-only sales page. ~$200-500K Year-1 at the current podcast and audience growth rate.

Future pace (6 months from now)

~$90-240K in premium-tier revenue not captured, plus the application page is still asking the warmest list in the audience to read long-form text and apply cold.

Fix

  • 6-10 minute homepage VSL on chrisharder.me, Chris and Lori on camera, generosity-coded register, ends at scorecard not at apply button
  • 4-6 minute application-page VSL on mastermindharder.com (EEM), focused on the EEM room composition + format + outcomes
  • 4-6 minute application-page VSL on chrisharder.me/mentor (MCM), focused on the MCM cadence + expert-coach office hours + the AI Clone Coaching layer
  • All three VSLs use the same shoot day, same B-roll bank, recycled clips across surfaces

Year-1 impact

$200-500K incremental on top of current EEM, MCM, and 1-on-1 revenue. Compounds with audience growth.

Bottleneck Two: Scorecard Pre-Application Replacing Cold APPLY NOW Buttons

What is happening now

Every paid program above $1K (MCM, EEM, VIP Weekend, 1-on-1) terminates at a raw "APPLY NOW" button with no qualifier. No revenue range gate, no role gate, no primary-bottleneck gate. Application volume from outside reads as all-comers via raw click. The team that handles applications has to do every layer of filtering manually downstream, which slows the sales conversation and lets tyre-kickers consume the same time slot as fit applicants.

Why it matters

A 12-question scorecard ("Money Multiplier Score" working name, can lock with Chris on Day 1) returns a 0-100 score plus a 3-line readout plus auto-segments the applicant to MCM-tier, EEM-tier, 1-on-1 tier, or strategy-session tier on submit. The scorecard does the first round of qualifying before the application reaches the team, and the email captured from the top of the scorecard funnel grows the list with already-segmented subscribers (not raw newsletter signups).

Cost of waiting

~$7-20K per month in EEM application close-rate friction (10-12% close on raw apps vs 22-28% on scorecard-segmented apps). ~$80-220K Year-1 plus the email list compounds with each scorecard impression that doesn't apply.

Future pace (6 months from now)

~$40-120K in EEM close-rate revenue not captured, plus the email list is 6 months smaller than it should be when the next MCM cohort opens.

Fix

  • 12-question Money Multiplier Score on Typeform or ScoreApp
  • Questions cover: current revenue band, primary offer type, primary bottleneck (offer, traffic, conversion, fulfilment, mindset), team size, growth ceiling, time-to-decision, mastermind history, content cadence
  • Output: 0-100 score, 3-line bottleneck readout, auto-routing to MCM apply / EEM apply / 1-on-1 apply / strategy-session apply based on score band
  • Email captured at the top of the scorecard, auto-tagged to the segmented welcome flow (see Bottleneck Three)

Year-1 impact

$80-220K from EEM application close-rate lift alone. Email list growth is additional, conservatively 4-7% IG-to-email on a high-intent business-coach audience = 2-4K incremental segmented subscribers in 90 days.

Bottleneck Three: Segmented Welcome Flow Replacing The Single Newsletter

What is happening now

Every email opt-in (blueprint download, newsletter signup, podcast subscribe, scorecard submit if it existed) currently lands on the same broadcast list. "Add some abundance to your inbox" is one lane for everyone. No revenue-band segmentation, no buyer-intent segmentation, no primary-bottleneck segmentation. The under-$25K beginner reads the same email as the $500K-plus EEM-fit operator.

Why it matters

The same email volume against three segmented lanes generates 2-4x the application volume per cycle. Lane A (under $25K) reads course-nurture content and converts on BeOnline or Money Principles. Lane B ($25K-$500K) reads MCM-fit content and is primed for the next MCM open. Lane C ($500K+) reads EEM-fit content and is primed for the next EEM intake. Each lane is right-cadence and right-offer for the subscriber's actual revenue band.

Cost of waiting

~$10-25K per month in unsegmented-newsletter conversion drag. ~$120-300K Year-1 compounding for life of the list.

Future pace (6 months from now)

~$60-150K in nurture-driven application volume not captured. Plus the list is 6 months untrained, so the first segmented send hits a list that has been broadcast-trained, which compresses early conversion lift.

Fix

  • 3-lane segmented welcome flow built on ConvertKit or whichever ESP currently runs the list
  • Lane A under $25K: 14-day BeOnline + Money Principles nurture, ending in a course-purchase ask
  • Lane B $25K-$500K: 14-day MCM-fit nurture (expert-coach office hours story, AI Clone Coaching demo, member transformations), ending in scorecard prompt or MCM application prompt
  • Lane C $500K+: 14-day EEM-fit nurture (in-person event story, quarterly 1-on-1 framing, 6-7-8-figure room composition), ending in scorecard prompt or EEM application prompt
  • Auto-routing from scorecard output, blueprint download tag, podcast-subscribe tag

Year-1 impact

$120-300K incremental from the same email volume converting at higher per-lane rates. Compounds for life of the list.

Bottleneck Four: Mid-Tier Holding-Pattern Offer For MCM Waitlist Windows

What is happening now

When MCM enrolment closes between cohorts, the warm list has no paid micro-offer to convert against. Every email opt-in landing during a waitlist window either books a one-off 90-min Strategy Session (the only paid option visible) or sits on broadcast newsletter until MCM reopens. The slice of the warm list that would convert on a $497-$997 micro-offer in the meantime is lost to inertia, and the warm list cools faster than it grows during dark months.

Why it matters

A productised 4-week implementation sprint at $497-$997, capped at 50 seats, sold quarterly during MCM-closed windows, holds the warm list with a paid commitment + a delivered outcome + a natural pre-qualifier for the next MCM cohort. Sprint grads convert into MCM at a higher rate than cold MCM applicants because they have already paid Chris and Lori once and have already done the implementation work.

Cost of waiting

~$10-20K per cycle window in micro-offer revenue not captured. Two to three cycles per year. ~$100-220K Year-1 plus the soft cost of warm-list cooling during dark months.

Future pace (6 months from now)

~$50-110K in sprint revenue not captured plus the warm list cools one full cycle window.

Fix

  • 4-week implementation sprint at $497-$997, capped at 50 seats per cycle
  • Sold quarterly during MCM-closed windows, two-week pre-sell from the warm list
  • Cadence: weekly live workshop with Chris or Lori, weekly office hours, private Skool community channel for the 4 weeks
  • Sprint grads get a fast-track invite into the next MCM cohort
  • The same scorecard from Bottleneck Two pre-qualifies sprint applicants

Year-1 impact

$100-220K from 2-3 sprint cycles per year. Plus the MCM cohort that opens after a sprint cycle fills faster because the warm-list nurture stayed paid-warm instead of broadcast-cold.

Results vary, dependent on application volume, cohort fill rate, podcast download growth, and operator inputs (Chris and Lori on-camera availability for the VSL shoot day, ESP migration scope if the current list lives on Mailchimp vs ConvertKit, scorecard length and question quality).

The infrastructure layers do not change the offer ladder. They do not remove the application gate on EEM or 1-on-1. They do not change the price points on MCM or the courses. They sit on top of what is already live and convert warmer.

What This Does Not Promise

  • Does not promise that EEM will fill faster or close at any particular rate. Application close rate is a function of Chris's call cadence, sales conversation, and qualification criteria; the scorecard only filters earlier.
  • Does not promise that the holding-pattern sprint will reach the seat cap on cycle one. First sprint cycle is a calibration cycle.
  • Does not promise that the VSL bridge replaces the role of the podcast. The podcast is the indoctrination engine; the VSL is the buy-decision bridge.
  • Does not promise that the segmented welcome flow will keep pace with audience growth without ongoing iteration. Segmentation is a quarterly review, not a set-and-forget.

The floors are conservative against Chris's current observable audience scale (243K IG, multi-year podcast catalogue, mature offer ladder). The ceilings assume Chris and Lori are bought in on the VSL shoot day, the scorecard goes live in week 1, the segmented flow ships in week 2, and the first sprint cycle runs inside 60 days of install.
