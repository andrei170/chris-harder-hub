The Four Things Missing (Ranked By Revenue Impact)

These map directly to the AG1 service catalogue, infrastructure layers on top of existing offers, not new offers.

  1. Podcast-to-application VSL bridge on EEM, MCM, and the homepage. Three text-only sales pages with one CTA each, into a podcast-trained audience that has spent years listening to Chris and Lori. A 6-10 minute homepage VSL plus embedded application-page VSLs from Chris and Lori (the two voices the audience already hears every week) bridges podcast trust into the buying decision. None of the peer-tier creators (Burchard, Porterfield, Graziosi) ship pure-text premium-tier sales pages. Year-1 floor on this lever alone: $200-500K incremental, with the highest lift on EEM where the application barrier is highest.

  2. Scorecard pre-application replacing cold APPLY NOW buttons. Every paid program above $1K terminates at a raw "APPLY NOW" form with no qualifier. A 12-question scorecard ("Money Multiplier Score" or "Mastermind Readiness Score") returns a 0-100 score plus a 3-line readout plus auto-segments the applicant to MCM-tier, EEM-tier, or 1-on-1 tier on submit. This filters tyre-kickers out before the application reaches Chris's team and pre-qualifies fit before the sales conversation. Year-1 floor: $80-220K from EEM application close-rate lift (10% to 25% on segmented apps vs raw apps) plus the email list growth from the scorecard top-funnel.

  3. Segmented welcome flow replacing the single newsletter. Currently every email opt-in (blueprint download, newsletter signup, podcast subscribe) lands on the same broadcast list. No tier segmentation, no buyer-intent segmentation, no primary-bottleneck segmentation. A 3-lane segmented welcome flow (Lane A under $25K = course nurture, Lane B $25K-$500K = MCM nurture, Lane C $500K+ = EEM nurture) routes each subscriber to the right offer in the right cadence. Year-1 floor: $120-300K, compounds for life of the list.

  4. Mid-tier holding-pattern offer for MCM waitlist windows. When MCM enrolment closes between cohorts, the warm list has no paid micro-offer to convert against. A $497-$997 implementation sprint (4-week paid live workshop, capped at 50, sold quarterly during MCM-closed windows) holds the warm list and pre-qualifies for MCM re-open. Year-1 floor: $100-220K from 2-3 sprint cycles per year.

Combined Year-1 incremental floor: $500K-$1.24M on top of existing MCM, EEM, 1-on-1, course, and Strategy Session revenue.

Results vary, dependent on application volume, cohort fill rate, and operator inputs.
