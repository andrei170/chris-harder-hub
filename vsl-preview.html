Phase 1

Chris Harder | VSL Preview Script

Prepared 2026-05-16 by AG1 Consulting Ltd for @chriswharder

Three VSLs ship in the install week. All three are shot in a single day with Chris and Lori. This doc has the 90-second preview cut + production notes + the full 5-10 minute custom scripts for the homepage, the EEM apply page, and the MCM apply page.

VSL One: Homepage VSL (6-10 minutes, Chris + Lori)

Function

Bridge podcast trust into the scorecard. The audience landing on chrisharder.me has heard hours of the show. The VSL says "the room is the install version of the podcast, here is the door, the scorecard tells you which room."

90-second preview cut (for the cold-traffic ad version)

Open (Chris, on camera, mid-shot, generosity-coded warm register):

"If you have been listening to The Chris Harder Show for a while, you already know the philosophy. Good people make good money so they can do great things. The podcast is the relationship. The next layer is the room."

Reframe (Lori cuts in, two-shot with Chris):

"There is a difference between hearing the philosophy every week and being in the room where the work happens. The room is where the mentorship is. It is where Chris and I are on the call, every other week. It is where the expert coaches sit office hours. It is where the implementation actually lands."

The bridge (Chris, slower cadence):

"Twelve questions. A 0-100 Money Multiplier Score. Three lines on what to fix first. That is the bridge between the podcast and the room. You take the score, the score tells you which room is the right next step, and the application gets routed for you."

Close (both, on the button):

"Take the score. Three minutes. Below this video."

Full 6-10 minute homepage VSL spec

Beat Length Who Function
1. Hook 30-45s Chris "If you have been listening to the show for a while..." establishes the audience-already-here premise
2. The philosophy lock 45-60s Chris + Lori "Good people, good money, great things." Reaffirm the brand, no new pitch
3. The gap (podcast vs room) 60-90s Lori The honest distinction: the podcast is the philosophy + the relationship, the room is the implementation cadence
4. The room composition 60-90s Chris What is actually inside MCM and EEM (Chris + Lori bi-weekly, expert coaches office hours, in-person events for EEM, AI Clone for 24/7 access)
5. The application question 45-60s Chris Why every paid program above $1K is by application (room composition is the product, the application protects the room)
6. The scorecard 90-120s Chris + Lori The Money Multiplier Score is the bridge. 12 questions, 0-100 score, 3-line readout, auto-routes to the right room
7. The Frello acknowledgement 20-30s Chris (optional, brand stretch goal) "I also co-built a peer-to-peer lending app called Frello. The coaching brand and the venture brand are separate; this funnel is the coaching brand." Keeps the credential clean without foregrounding
8. Close 30-45s Both Take the score, three minutes, button below

Total runtime: 6:20 to 9:30 depending on Chris's cadence.

Production notes

  • Setting: Chris and Lori's home office or the Harder Studio backdrop (whichever is the current podcast background). Audience already associates the background with them.
  • Wardrobe: brand-typical, warm tones, Lori in her usual on-camera palette, Chris in dark casual.
  • Camera: single mid-shot per person, two-shot for the duo segments. No moving camera, no jib, no drone. Podcast-native production language.
  • Sound: lavaliers on both. Music bed light, ducks under voice, generosity-coded warm string pad or similar.
  • B-roll bank to capture same day: podcast studio shots, EEM in-person event archive footage if available, AI Clone Coaching screen-record, scorecard-on-phone shot, blueprint PDF on iPad, "Add some abundance to your inbox" newsletter on phone.
  • Lower thirds: Chris Harder, Founder + Host. Lori Harder, Co-Host + Founder.
  • CTA card at end of VSL: "Take The Money Multiplier Score" button graphic, scorecard URL underneath.

VSL Two: EEM Apply-Page VSL (4-6 minutes, Chris + Lori)

Function

Sit on mastermindharder.com (the EEM apply page) above the application form. The viewer has already taken the scorecard and scored Lane C ($500K+), or has navigated direct from the homepage with high intent. The VSL closes the gap between "I am thinking about EEM" and "I am clicking apply now."

Script outline

Beat Length Who Function
1. Audience lock 20-30s Chris "If you are here, you are scaling past seven figures or you are sitting at the high six-figure ceiling..." names the avatar
2. The EEM room composition 60-90s Chris + Lori 6, 7, and 8-figure entrepreneurs in the same room. Three in-person events per year. Bi-weekly group calls. Quarterly 1-on-1. Monthly EEM-only mastermind calls.
3. Why the application gate 30-45s Chris Room composition is the product. The application is the protection of the room composition, not a sales filter.
4. What a typical EEM operator looks like 45-60s Lori Stays at the avatar level, no named member without permission. Operator profile: coaching, agency, SaaS, info product, ecom, the room is industry-agnostic, $500K+ is the floor.
5. The 3-event year 30-45s Chris The in-person events anchor the year. The bi-weekly calls keep the room together between events.
6. The application question 30-45s Chris "Application is four minutes. We review and respond within 5 business days. If the room is the right next step for you, we will get on a call."
7. Close 20-30s Both Apply form below.

Total runtime: 4:00 to 5:30.

Production notes

  • Shot in the same day as VSL One, same setting, same wardrobe.
  • Slightly more formal register than the homepage VSL. EEM is the highest-stakes room.
  • B-roll bank: EEM event archive footage (with permission), Chris and Lori at past events, the room composition stills.

VSL Three: MCM Apply-Page VSL (4-6 minutes, Chris + Lori)

Function

Sit on chrisharder.me/mentor (the MCM apply page) above the application form. The viewer has scored Lane B ($25K-$500K), or has navigated from the homepage. The VSL closes the gap between "MCM looks interesting" and "I am applying for the next cohort."

Script outline

Beat Length Who Function
1. Audience lock 20-30s Chris "If you are between $25K and $500K and you are looking for the room before EEM..." names the avatar
2. The MCM cadence 60-90s Chris + Lori Bi-weekly group calls with Chris and Lori. Weekly expert-coach office hours. Unlimited coaching. The AI Clone for 24/7 access.
3. The expert-coach layer 45-60s Lori What office hours actually look like. The depth of the seven-coach team, what each specialises in, why this layer is the differentiator.
4. The AI Clone Coaching 30-45s Chris The Clone is trained on the catalogue. It does not replace the room. It extends access to between-call hours.
5. The cohort cycle 30-45s Chris MCM opens on cycle. The application is the gate. The sprint is the alternative when MCM is closed (cross-link to sprint page).
6. Application question 30-45s Chris "Application is four minutes. The cohort is capped to keep the room intimate."
7. Close 20-30s Both Apply form below.

Total runtime: 4:00 to 5:30.

Production notes

  • Shot in the same day as VSL One and Two.
  • Warmer register than EEM, MCM is the on-ramp room, the voice is more welcoming.
  • B-roll bank: expert-coach headshots, AI Clone Coaching demo screen, MCM Skool community screen, member transformation receipts (with permission) for the wall.

Cross-VSL Production Day Plan

Single day, three VSLs, one B-roll bank.

Block Time What
08:00-09:00 Setup Lights, sound check, wardrobe
09:00-10:30 Homepage VSL 2-3 takes per beat, A-roll capture
10:30-12:00 EEM VSL 2-3 takes per beat
12:00-13:00 Lunch
13:00-14:30 MCM VSL 2-3 takes per beat
14:30-16:00 B-roll Studio shots, screen-records (scorecard, AI Clone, blueprint, segmented welcome flow), podcast-studio reverse shots
16:00-17:00 Wrap Final pickup shots, lower-thirds graphics direction, music brief

Cuts delivered Day 3 of the install week. Embedded on the three sales pages Day 3-4. Homepage VSL goes live on day of embed; apply-page VSLs go live with the scorecard launch.

What This VSL Set Does Not Include

  • A long-form (15-30 min) "story of Chris Harder" VSL. The about page handles biography; the apply-page VSLs are conversion-focused, not biography-focused.
  • A Frello-specific VSL. Frello is a separate company, separate funnel. Cross-reference at the credential level on the homepage VSL only, no dedicated cut.
  • A sprint sales-page VSL (the sprint sales page uses a 90-second teaser cut from the homepage VSL plus a 3-minute "what the sprint is" Loom recorded separately and faster, not a full studio VSL shoot).
  • A podcast-episode-trailer cut for the show itself (the podcast already runs evergreen, separate workstream).

The three VSLs in this doc cover homepage, EEM, and MCM. Sprint and 1-on-1 ride on lighter assets so the install week ships at the less-than-7-day target.
