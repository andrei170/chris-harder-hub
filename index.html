Questions

The Ones An Operator At Your Stage Asks

Short, direct answers. The longer ones we cover on the call.

Why would I run this with an outside operator instead of in-house?

Because the team you have is already running the business. The podcast is shipping weekly. EEM and MCM are running. The 1-on-1 is running. Frello is in market. Adding a 90-day VSL build, a CEO Legacy Room launch, a micro-offer, and a paid acquisition layer on top of an already-loaded team is how those projects either get postponed by a year or get launched at half the quality. Outside operator is faster, cleaner, and exits when production hits steady state. We hand the system to your team after the launch cycle, not before.

How much of my time does this need?

One day to film the homepage VSL. One day to film the EEM application-page VSL. Calendar holds for the Legacy Room intake. The rest is build team work plus you and Lori reviewing the copy, creative, and pricing decisions before they ship. Most operators at your stage spend under 8 hours a month during the 90-day build, and under 2 hours a month after the system is in production.

Will the CEO Legacy Room cannibalise EEM?

No. EEM and the Legacy Room solve different problems. EEM is the room for $500K+ operators scaling past 7 figures: build the team, build the recurring revenue, scale into the next bracket. The Legacy Room is the room for 8-figure operators inside a real capital decision window: exit / hold / compound on the operating asset, capital allocation across the stack, family-office formation, generosity-as-strategy. Different deliverable, different room, different decade. Most Legacy Room members will be EEM graduates moving up the ladder, not net-new buyers choosing Legacy Room over EEM. The two rooms compound, they don't compete.

How does this work alongside the existing 1-on-1?

The 1-on-1 with you is private coaching for established businesses. The Legacy Room is peer-room access for 8-figure operators with structured 12-month implementation plus quarterly 1-on-1s with you or Lori. Different format, different price, different deliverable. The Legacy Room sits between EEM and the private 1-on-1 on the ladder, capturing the operator who wants more than EEM but isn't a private-1-on-1 fit. Where applicable, Legacy Room graduates can elect into the 1-on-1 afterwards, completing the ladder.

What about Lori's role across the build?

Lori co-anchors the existing rooms, so she co-anchors the Legacy Room. The cohort dinners, the quarterly 1-on-1s, the bi-weekly group calls all run with both of you. The featured guest spot on Lori's podcast is included in the Legacy Room benefits, the same way it currently sits inside EEM. The build doesn't change the partnership architecture; it just builds a senior tier that uses both of you the way EEM and MCM already do.

How quickly is there real revenue?

Lever 01 VSLs on the homepage and EEM page: first measurable conversion lift in week 4-6 once the pages ship. Lever 03 micro-offer: launched into the next MCM waitlist window, captures revenue from week one of the window onward. Lever 02 first Legacy Room cohort sells in month 2-3, runs in month 3-4. What if the Legacy Room doesn't fill?

The Legacy Room is sized at 6-8 seats first, sold to existing EEM alumni (the warmest list for this offer that exists). If the first intake under-fills (say, 4 confirmed instead of 6), we don't push it into a half-empty room. We pause, sharpen the positioning and the application screen, push the date by 30-45 days, and try again with the second wave of alumni outreach plus any external warm sources we can verify. Build doesn't continue billing while the cohort isn't running. If after a second attempt the audience isn't there at the modelled price, we restructure the tier or move the budget into Lever 03 paid acquisition.

What if I want to stop after a Lever?

You can stop at any Lever. After Lever 01 you own both VSLs and the segmented nurture as standalone lifts on your existing funnel. After Lever 02 you own the Legacy Room sales page, application flow, 12-month curriculum, and the first cohort revenue. After Lever 03 you own the micro-offer and the paid acquisition system. Each Lever stands on its own as a fully shipped asset in case the rest stops being interesting to you.

The work here is precise: VSLs on the homepage and EEM page, a micro-offer holding the MCM waitlist, a senior CEO Legacy Room launched off EEM alumni, a paid layer on top of an organic engine that already converts.
